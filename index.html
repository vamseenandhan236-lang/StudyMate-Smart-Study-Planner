The Pomodoro Method

  • 1 Pick one study topic or assignment from your task list.
  • 2 Focus exclusively for 25 minutes without checking phone or social media.
  • 3 Take a 5-minute breather: stretch, drink water, rest eyes.
  • 4 After 4 sessions, reward yourself with a rejuvenating 15-minute break.
